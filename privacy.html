Privacy Policy

At GD Demon Hub, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This document outlines the types of information collected.

Log Files

Like many other websites, we make use of log files. These files merely log visitors to the site—usually a standard procedure for hosting companies and a part of hosting services' analytics.

Cookies and Web Beacons

GD Demon Hub uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and to record user-specific information on which pages the site visitor accesses or visits.

Third-Party Ads

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ad Settings.
