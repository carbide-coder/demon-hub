Welcome to the Demon Hub

Since the introduction of the Demon difficulty in Update 1.2, the Geometry Dash community has transformed from a casual mobile audience into a high-performance e-sports ecosystem. Our mission is to document the hardest levels in existence and provide players with a clear roadmap for skill progression.

Whether you are jumping into your first Demon or grinding a Top 10 Extreme, this hub provides the level IDs and context you need to succeed.

Easy Demons

Easy Demons are the gateway to competitive play. These levels typically feature basic "fake" paths and require a high mastery of the Cube and Ship game modes. Most players begin their journey with 1.2-era levels before moving into modern 2.2 platformers.

Medium & Hard Demons

This tier introduces complex Boss Fights and the "Nine Circles" wave style. Consistency becomes the main focus here. Players must learn to handle high-speed transitions and memorization-heavy "memory" sections that can span the entire length of the level.

  1. B (Medium Demon) #34654271
  2. Nine Circles (Hard Demon) #4215362
  3. Future Funk (Hard Demon XL) #45123654

Extreme Demons

The "Main List." These levels represent the absolute ceiling of human reaction speed. Levels like Tidal Wave and Avernus require thousands—sometimes hundreds of thousands—of attempts to master. Frame-perfect clicks (clicking within a 1/60th of a second window) are common in this tier.

  1. Tidal Wave (Current Top 1) #95123412
  2. Acu (Best First Extreme) #61234123
  3. Bloodbath (Legacy Legend) #10563421

Core Mechanics & Progression

To advance through the Demon tiers, players must master several high-level mechanics:

  • Straight Flying: Controlled micro-clicks to keep the ship in a perfectly horizontal line.
  • Wave Spacing: Precise zig-zag movements through tight gaps, often at 3x or 4x speed.
  • Dual Control: Managing two separate icons simultaneously, often performing different tasks on each side of the screen.
  • Jitter Clicking: High-frequency clicking techniques used for rapid-fire game modes like the UFO or Spiders.

Community History: The Demon List

The "Demon List" was originally a forum-based project to track the hardest levels, but it has since evolved into a massive community-run website with strict record-submission rules. To have a completion recognized, players must provide video proof with audible clicks and "cheat indicator" mods to ensure fair play.

Legendary players like Riot, Sunix, and Zoink have defined different eras of the game, each pushing the "impossible" barrier further than the last. As of Update 2.2, the introduction of the Camera Trigger and Swing Copter has added a new layer of complexity to future Extremes.

Hardware Matters

Polling Rate: Gaming mice with a 1000Hz+ polling rate reduce input lag, which is vital for frame-perfect jumps.

Megahack: Most top players use the Megahack toolset for practice features like StartPos Switcher and Hitbox Visualization.
