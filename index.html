The History of the Demon List

Geometry Dash was first released with only standard difficulty ratings. It wasn't until the community began creating "impossible" levels that the concept of a Demon was born. Early levels like Demon Park set the standard, proving that players were hungry for higher challenges.

As the game entered the 2.0 and 2.1 eras, decoration became as important as difficulty. This led to a split in the community: those who built for aesthetics, and those who built to create the next hardest level in existence. Today, the "Demon List" is a globally recognized ranking that tracks the absolute limit of human reaction speed and consistency.

Pro Tips

Refresh Rate: High refresh rates (144hz+) are nearly essential for Extreme Demon wave sections.

Practice: Always use Start Positions to master the final 20% of a level first.
